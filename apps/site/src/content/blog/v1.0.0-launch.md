# Silent Mode Control 1.0 Launched on Google Play

We are thrilled to announce the official release of **Silent Mode Control 1.0** on the Google Play Store!

For years, Android users have faced an all-or-nothing dilemma: either silence your phone completely and risk missing critical calls, or leave the ringer on and endure robocalls, telemarketers, and work notifications during dinner or sleep.

**Silent Mode Control solves this problem with surgical precision.**

---

## What Makes Silent Mode Control Different?

Most "Do Not Disturb" tools only offer basic starred contact exemptions or global quiet hours. Silent Mode Control gives you full, granular control down to the individual minute and specific caller.

### Key Highlights of Version 1.0

1. **Individual Contact Precedence:**
   - Define custom ringer schedules for specific numbers (e.g., your child's school, emergency doctor, close family).
   - Individual contact rules **always override group settings**, giving you total certainty.

2. **Smart Contact Groups:**
   - Group contacts into *Family*, *Work Colleagues*, or *VIPs*.
   - Apply group-level fallback rules to easily manage entire sets of contacts at once.

3. **Graphical 24-Hour Timeline Bar:**
   - A visual, color-coded timeline lets you see your schedule across the entire 24-hour day:
     - **Ring & Vibrate** (Green)
     - **Ring Only** (Blue)
     - **Vibrate Only** (Orange)
     - **Silent / Mute** (Purple)
     - **Block Call** (Red)

4. **Native Android Telecom Integration:**
   - Built on Android 10+ `CallScreeningService`.
   - Incoming calls are screened in real time by the OS without running battery-draining background polling loops.

5. **100% Privacy by Design:**
   - Operates entirely on-device. No accounts, no cloud servers, and zero third-party telemetry.
